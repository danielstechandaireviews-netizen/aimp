## Self-paced courses

You may request a full refund within **14 days** of purchase, provided you have completed less than **30%** of the course content.

## Live cohorts

Full refund if you cancel more than **7 days** before the cohort starts. After that, you may transfer to a future cohort **once, free of charge**.

## 1-on-1 mentorship

Sessions can be rescheduled with at least **24 hours'** notice. Unused sessions in a package are refundable within **30 days** of purchase and **non-refundable** after that.

## Community training and business services

Cancellation and refund terms are set out in your proposal or agreement.

## How to request a refund

Email **daniel@aimonipoint.com** with your name, order details and the reason for your request. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within **10 business days**.
