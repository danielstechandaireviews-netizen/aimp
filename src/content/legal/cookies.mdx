## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We also use similar technologies such as local storage.

## How we use them

| Category | Purpose | When used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Essential** | Remember your cookie choice, theme (light/dark) and display currency | Always — needed for the site to work as you expect |
| **Analytics** | Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it (Google Analytics) | Only if you click **Accept all** |
| **Embedded content** | The Calendly booking calendar sets its own cookies | Only if you accept, or when you choose to load the calendar |

## Your choices

When you first visit, you can **accept** or **reject** non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time using the **Cookie settings** link in the footer. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
