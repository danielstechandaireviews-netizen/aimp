## From demo to production

A RAG prototype can be built in an afternoon. Making it **accurate, secure and maintainable** on messy real-world documents is a different job — and that's what this course is about.

You'll work with realistic document sets, measure retrieval quality with a proper evaluation set, and learn the techniques that consistently improve answers: better chunking, hybrid search, re-ranking and grounded citations.

<Callout>
**Bring your own data:** live-cohort students can apply the techniques to their own (non-confidential) documents during the capstone.
</Callout>
