## Why this bootcamp

Most AI tutorials stop at a chat window. Real AI products need much more: clean APIs, reliable data access, sensible prompts, cost control and a way to tell whether the output is actually good.

This bootcamp teaches AI the way it is built in industry — as **one component inside a well-engineered system**. Every module ends with working code, and by the final week you'll have shipped a deployed AI application you can show employers or clients.

## How it works

- **Weekly modules** with short videos, written guides and code
- **Hands-on labs** you complete in your own repository
- **Live cohort option** with weekly Q&A and code reviews
- **Capstone project** of your choosing, deployed to the cloud
