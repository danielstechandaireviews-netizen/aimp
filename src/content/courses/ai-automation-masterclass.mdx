## Built for busy professionals

You don't need to be a developer to save hours every week with AI. This masterclass shows you how to look at any business process, find the repetitive parts, and automate them with visual tools and AI — safely, with people kept in control.

By the end you'll have **three working automations** and a simple framework for finding and pricing many more — for your own business or for clients.
